# Moonlit Tide

Create an original art-direction board for a top-down exploration and
village-management pixel game named Realmseed.

Palette: deep navy ground, blue-green foliage, turquoise water, lavender stone,
coral, and pale-gold highlights. Show a large top-down magical coastal scene
with islands, forest pockets, village docks, compact houses, shrine ruins, a
luminous ancient waystone, and a clear fog-of-war boundary. Also show four
distinct travelers/fishers, three monsters, and several buildings and props.

Use true low-resolution pixel art based on a disciplined 24x24 or 32x32 grid:
deliberate square pixel clusters, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, flat stepped
shadows, strong silhouettes, and readability at tiny size. Clean board without
labels or text. Original designs only.

Avoid smooth digital painting, gradients, blur, isometric or side views, 3D,
photorealism, tiny illegible details, logos, signatures, text, and watermarks.
